# Information Security Policy

Everyone at Contoso Lattice protects customer and company data. These rules apply to every device and account used for work.

## Accounts and authentication

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory on every company account. Use a passphrase of at least 14 characters, unique to each account, stored in the company password manager. Never share an account or a one-time code with anyone, including colleagues from IT.

## Devices

Company laptops are encrypted and managed. Lock your screen when you leave your desk; screens lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. USB storage devices are blocked on company laptops.

## Data classification

Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Confidential and Restricted data must not be pasted into external tools, personal e-mail or unapproved AI services.

## Reporting phishing and incidents

Report a suspicious e-mail with the Report Phishing button in the mail client. Report a lost device or a suspected security incident to the Security Desk within 1 hour of noticing it, at any time of day.
