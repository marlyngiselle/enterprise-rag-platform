# Quarter Close Procedure

## Close calendar

The quarter close runs over 6 working days. Accruals are due on working day 3, intercompany reconciliations on working day 4, and the consolidated report is published on working day 6.

## Purchase orders

Purchase orders above 5,000 EUR need two approvals: the budget owner and a finance controller. Purchase orders above 50,000 EUR also need approval from the chief financial officer.

## Accruals

Every accrual above 10,000 EUR needs supporting evidence, such as a signed contract or a statement of work, attached in the finance system.
