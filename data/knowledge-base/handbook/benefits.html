Employee Benefits

Contoso Lattice benefits apply to every employee with a permanent contract, from the first day.

Health insurance

The company pays 80 percent of the premium for the standard health plan, for you and your dependants. You can upgrade to the premium plan and pay the difference through payroll.

Pension

Contoso Lattice matches your pension contributions up to 5 percent of your base salary. Enrolment is automatic and you can change your contribution once per quarter.

Learning budget

Every employee has a learning budget of 1,000 EUR per year for courses, certifications, books and conferences. Unused budget does not roll over.
