# Leave and Time Off

Contoso Lattice wants people to rest. This policy explains how much leave you have, how to request it and what happens to days you do not use.

## Annual leave

Every full-time employee receives 27 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, on top of public holidays. Part-time employees receive leave in proportion to their contracted hours.

### Requesting leave

Request leave in the Harbor HR portal. Requests of more than three consecutive days need at least 10 working days of notice and approval from your manager. Managers answer requests within five working days.

### Carry over

You may carry over up to 5 unused days into the next year. Carried over days must be used before 31 March, after which they expire. Unused days beyond the carry over limit are not paid out, except where local law requires it.

## Sick leave

Tell your manager on the first day of absence. You can self-certify sickness for up to 3 consecutive calendar days. From the fourth day a medical certificate is required, uploaded to Harbor.

## Parental leave

The primary caregiver receives 20 weeks of fully paid parental leave. The secondary caregiver receives 6 weeks of fully paid parental leave, which can be taken in up to three blocks within the first year after the birth or adoption.

## Public holidays

Public holidays follow the country of your employment contract. The list for each country is published in Harbor at the start of the year.
