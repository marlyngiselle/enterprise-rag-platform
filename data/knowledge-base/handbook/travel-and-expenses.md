# Travel and Expenses

Spend company money as if it were your own. This policy sets the limits for business travel and the rules for claiming expenses.

## Booking travel

Book flights, trains and hotels through the Compass travel tool. Train is preferred for journeys under four hours. Flights under 6 hours are booked in economy class; longer flights may be booked in premium economy.

## Hotels

The hotel cap is 180 EUR per night. In capital cities the cap is 250 EUR per night. Stays above the cap need written approval from your manager before booking.

## Meals and per diem

A daily per diem of 45 EUR covers meals and small costs when you travel overnight. Client dinners are reimbursed at actual cost with the names of attendees.

## Submitting expenses

Submit expense claims in Compass within 30 days of the expense. Attach a receipt for every expense above 25 EUR. Your manager approves claims, and approved claims are reimbursed with the next monthly salary.
