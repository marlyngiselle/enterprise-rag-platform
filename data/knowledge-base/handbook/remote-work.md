# Remote and Hybrid Work

Contoso Lattice is hybrid by default. Teams agree their rhythm, within the limits below.

## Office presence

Employees attached to an office spend at least 2 days per week on site. Teams choose the days together and record them in the team charter.

## Core hours

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 Central European Time. Meetings involving more than one team should be scheduled inside core hours.

## Home office equipment

New employees receive a one-time home office stipend of 600 EUR for a desk, a chair or a screen. An internet allowance of 30 EUR per month is paid with the salary to anyone working from home at least one day per week.

## Working from abroad

You may work from another country for up to 20 working days per calendar year. Each stay needs approval from your manager and from People Operations at least two weeks before travel, because of tax and social security rules.
