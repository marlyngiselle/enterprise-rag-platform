# Compensation Review 2026

Restricted. Shared only with the Total Rewards team and HR business partners.

## Merit budget

The merit increase budget for 2026 is 3.5 percent of the base salary payroll. Managers propose increases between 0 and 8 percent, and the average for each department must stay within the budget.

## Salary bands

Engineering salary bands for 2026 are: level 1 from 48,000 to 58,000 EUR, level 2 from 56,000 to 70,000 EUR, level 3 from 68,000 to 86,000 EUR, level 4 from 84,000 to 105,000 EUR.

## Calendar

Managers submit proposals by 15 February. Increases take effect on 1 April and appear in the April payslip.
